Excited to share Version 0.2 of the HiLyst Unified Business Intelligence & Decision Intelligence Platform!

"Visualize. Analyze. Then Decide."

Multi-channel commerce brands face massive analytical fragmentation: disparate marketplace portals, uncoordinated cross-channel pricing, disconnected ad spend attribution, and inventory stockout margin bleed.

HiLyst bridges this gap by engineering an enterprise Medallion Data Warehouse on Microsoft SQL Server 2025 that integrates 13 multi-source datasets (559,000+ raw records) into a Kimball Star Schema with an interactive executive decision dashboard.

KEY ARCHITECTURAL HIGHLIGHTS:
- 13 Multi-Source Datasets Ingested: Amazon India B2C, Amazon Global Direct, Flipkart Quick-Commerce, International B2B Wholesale, Google Ads Paid Search, Meta Ads Social, Meta Lead Gen, Warehouse Stock, and Multi-Portal Pricing Catalogs.
- Kimball Star Schema: 8 Conformed Dimensions (DimDate, DimProduct, DimCustomer, DimChannel, DimFulfillment, DimLocation, DimMarketingCampaign, DimSeller) and 6 Fact Tables (FactSalesOrderItems with 515,187 sales lines).
- Automated Data Governance: 18-Test Data Quality Audit Suite executed directly on SQL Server with 100% Pass Rate (0 critical defects).
- Governed Semantic Layer: 10 pre-computed SQL views exposing Executive KPIs, Pareto 80/20, Customer RFM, Marketing ROAS, and Pricing Arbitrage.
- Autonomous AI Decision Engine: 4 prescriptive decision models providing root-cause diagnostics, quantified financial impact, and confidence scores.
- Interactive BI Dashboard: Zero-dependency web application built in an Obsidian Dark and Studio Light design system with professional vector iconography.
